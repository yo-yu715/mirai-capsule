About

Unletter lets you seal today’s feelings into a “special envelope” and deliver them to the future. Add a message, photos, and your voice, then seal the letter with a one-of-a-kind wax seal. Once you pick an open day, no one can see inside until that day arrives. One year, five years, ten years later — a notification reaches you on the day you chose.

FAQ

  • Can I change the open day after sealing a letter?
    Once a letter is sealed, its contents stay hidden until the open day. You can freely edit a letter any time before you seal it.
  • Where is my data stored? Does it survive switching phones?
    Your letters are stored on your device and in your private iCloud, and sync automatically across devices signed in with the same Apple ID. Even when you switch phones or reinstall, your letters to the future carry over. Your data is never sent or shared outside your account.
  • Is the wax seal different for each letter?
    Yes. Every time you seal a letter, a wax seal is born that is never the same twice in color or shape.
  • What’s the difference between Free and Pro?
    The free plan lets you create up to 3 letters, with 1 photo per letter. Pro unlocks unlimited letters, voice messages, and up to 4 photos per letter. One purchase, yours forever.
